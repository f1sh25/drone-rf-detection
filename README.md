# RF-Based UAV Signal Classification with Ensemble Learning and Convoluted Neural Networks  



## Introduction  


The war in Ukraine has demonstrated the increasing use of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) in modern warfare. Counter-UAV (C-UAV) technologies designed to detect and counter these threats are still in development due to the complex electromagnetic environment of the battlefield. In particular, detecting UAVs via their RF emissions is challenging because of signal clutter from telecommunications, radar systems, and electronic warfare jammers.  

Machine learning (ML) approaches are suited for this task, as they can learn complex signal patterns and maintain performance even in low signal-to-noise (SNR) conditions. In this report, we compare the performance of **Random Forests** and **Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)** for UAV detection across varying SNR levels.
